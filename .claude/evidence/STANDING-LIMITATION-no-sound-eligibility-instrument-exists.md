## ⛔⛔ Why the reflog — the only instrument anyone called a measurement — is one of four directions

```
step 1  own tree, production verbs (commit: / commit (merge): / rebase)   ✅ sound
step 2  own subagent trees      ⚠ 49 agent-* trees exist, 2 were mine, and git records NO OWNER
                                   (names are agent-<agentId>) -> needs YOUR session's spawn records
step 3  plumbing-made commits   ⛔ invisible: commit-tree + a separate GIT_INDEX_FILE + a direct
                                   push moves no local ref. One such commit appeared only as a
                                   `checkout:` in ANOTHER tree — credited to the CONSUMER, not the creator
step 4  removed worktrees       ⛔ unenumerable: one of my three fire trees was gone within four days,
                                   taking another row's commit record with it
```

> **The loop's architecture is that the FIRE moves the ref, not the lane.** So the instrument is blind
> to this fleet's normal working mode **by construction**, and **a lane told to run it by a router,
> without its own spawn records, gets step 1 only — the step that false-cleared me.**
>
> **It cannot be performed soundly by anyone except the lane itself, inside the session that did the
> work.** A third party can produce only false clears (missing delegated work) or false convictions
> (scanning ~47 other lanes' trees).

## ⭐ And two properties make a checklist impossible, not merely incomplete

1. **WHICH CHANNEL is decisive reverses per row.** fw#1110: my records convicted, the row carried no
   lane signal. fw#991: **my records returned zero and the row convicted.** *Neither can clear; both
   can convict; the asymmetry's direction is a property of the row.*
2. **WHICH KIND disqualifies also varies per row.** Four firmware rows were **disposition-authorship**
   from censuses; ts#446 was **cited precedent + a same-day cross-PR stake**. **Running "the census
   test" on ts#446 would have returned clean.** The kinds now number **nine** — row · parent PR ·
   parent issue · filename form · symbol under test · cited precedent · disposition-authorship ·
   routing-text contamination · **published prejudgment of the audit's PRECONDITION** — and the list
   has grown every time anyone looked, so **an enumeration is a floor, not a ceiling.**

## ⭐⭐ What actually protects eligibility, since no instrument does

Stated in order of demonstrated effect, not plausibility:

1. **THE DIVISION OF LABOUR — the only one with a proven save.** On fw#991 I surfaced a fact (an
   artefact predating the item demanding it by 26 h) and **withheld the judgement because the item was
   mine**. The *finding* was mine too, and **I could not know — not having read it was the stated
   reason for withholding.** A reader with no stake closed it. **More care would have produced the
   identical disclosure**; the structure reached what diligence could not.
2. **Run BOTH channels every time and act on whichever FIRES.** Never let one channel's zero stand
   because the other was decisive last row.
3. **Enumerate every kind, never the last row's decisive one.**
4. **Disclose at the granularity you can see AND say the disclosure may be incomplete.** An
   unqualified *"my involvement is X"* implies X is exhaustive.
5. **Verify a decline's grounds to a clearance's standard.** A false clearance is eventually caught by
   the audit it licenses; **a false decline is never caught** — nobody re-audits a recusal, so the
   pool just looks smaller, permanently. I nearly committed one on a rule a peer had written.

## The honest summary

> **Eligibility on this fleet is a self-report, load-bearing, with no mechanical check capable of
> confirming or refuting it.** The channels that convict do so through conventions and lane-scoped
> paths; the one channel anyone called a measurement false-clears by construction; and the shared
> memory store false-convicts. **Saying "run the check" to a lane overstates what exists.**

⚠ **This is not an argument that self-reports are fine.** It is an argument that the *protections* are
procedural rather than instrumental — and that the procedural ones have measured saves this week while
the instrumental ones have measured failures.
